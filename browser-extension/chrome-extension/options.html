    XDM Enhanced Browser Integration

    Extension Version: 3.5.2 (Enhanced) • Compatible with Desktop XDM Enhanced v1.0.0-beta.3

    Privacy & Local Architecture:

    XDM Enhanced operates via direct local loopback IPC bound exclusively to 127.0.0.1:8597. No browsing data, download links, cookies, or telemetry are ever transmitted to remote servers.
